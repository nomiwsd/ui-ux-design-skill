# Blueprint — Corporate / institutional

**Path:** `src/references/blueprints/corporate.md`
Section anatomy: `references/17-section-library.md` · Method: `blueprints/00-index.md`

Credibility over cleverness. But conventional *structure* does not require a default *execution* — a strict grid with real typographic discipline reads far more credible than another stock-photo-and-blue-gradient corporate page.

## Page inventory

| Page | Purpose | Primary action | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | Establish what the organization does and its scale | Enter a section | P0 |
| What we do / services | Make the offering legible | Contact | P0 |
| About / leadership | Establish who is accountable | — | P0 |
| Case studies / clients | Evidence | Contact | P1 |
| News / press | Prove activity | — | P1 |
| Careers | Recruit | Apply | P1 |
| Contact / locations | Route enquiries | Contact | P0 |
| Investors, compliance, policies | Obligation | Download | Varies |

## Home

Sections: a clear statement of what the organization does, in plain language, without abstraction ("We manage water infrastructure for 14 councils" beats "Delivering integrated solutions") · scale and proof (years, clients, coverage, accreditations) · routes into the three or four most-visited areas · recent activity · contact.

Institutional visitors are usually on a task — find a document, find a person, verify a claim, contact someone. Prioritize wayfinding over persuasion.

## Documents and PDFs

Frequently the most-used content and almost always the worst-handled.

Every document listing shows: title, format, file size, publication date, and language. Documents are tagged and searchable. PDFs are tagged and accessible, or an HTML version is offered alongside. Never link a PDF that opens without warning on mobile.

## Accessibility

Public-sector and many regulated organizations have a legal obligation (EU EAA, ADA, Section 508, WCAG 2.2 AA in most national procurement). This changes what can ship, not just what's preferred.

Required: an accessibility statement with a named contact and complaints route · verified keyboard operability across every flow · document accessibility · captions and transcripts on all media · plain-language content · no reliance on colour alone.

## Trust and governance

Leadership named with real photographs and real biographies · registration and company numbers · accreditations with dates · policies findable in two clicks · a physical address · named press and enquiry contacts.

## Style guidance

Conservative structure, considered execution. Restraint is the licence here, not blandness: a real type pairing, a warm or distinctly cool neutral ramp, hairline rules instead of card soup, and disciplined spacing will do more for perceived credibility than any decorative treatment. Motion at intensity 1–2.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] What the organization does is stated in plain language above the fold
- [ ] Contact routes are on every page
- [ ] No hierarchy deeper than three levels
- [ ] Documents show format, size, and date; PDFs are accessible or paired with HTML
- [ ] Accessibility statement present with a named contact
- [ ] Full keyboard operability verified across every flow
- [ ] Content passes plain-language review
- [ ] Works at 320px and 200% zoom
